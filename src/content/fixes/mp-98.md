The retro banner carries a "Back to modern" button. It is the exit the site promises on every retro page. Activating it switched the mode and announced "Modern mode on".

The banner then hid itself, with the focused button inside it. Focus fell to the page body. The next Tab started again from the top of the document, and some screen readers announced nothing or restarted reading.

The footer's appearance toggle does not have this problem, because it stays visible.

A keyboard or screen-reader visitor who wanted to leave retro mode and carry on reading lost their place. The banner exits on every page, so the fault was sitewide.

The exit handler now moves focus to the main content area after switching. After the exit, focus sits on a visible element, not the body.
