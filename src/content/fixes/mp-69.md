On a phone, the header's "more" menu opened a panel of links under the sticky header. Tapping outside it, scrolling, pressing Escape and tabbing past its last link all left it open. Only a second tap on "more" closed it.

The panel is 168 by 109 pixels. On the homepage it covered the right half of the "Matt Pyle" heading. After a scroll it covered the "all projects" link.

This let down anyone on a phone who opened the menu, decided against it and wanted to keep reading. It also let down keyboard users, who tabbed out of the panel and left it open over the page they had moved on to.

The cause was a plain details element, which closes only from its own summary. A small script now closes it on a tap outside, on scroll, when focus leaves it and on Escape. Escape returns focus to the summary.
