The Scorecard page explained when a run is logged, in the line under "Previous runs". It read: "A run is logged when a score moves enough to matter, or after a week without one." Since 15 November 2026 the scheduled run has published every night. The rows for 15 and 16 November both read "All 107 pages passed all four public metrics." Every score in them matched the 13 November row.

Neither row moved a score. Neither came a week after the row before it. By the page's own rule, they should not have been there. A returning reader who came back to see what had changed would look for the moved score and find none. The markdown version of the page carried the same sentence.

The fix replaced the sentence with the rule the log now follows. Every scheduled run is logged. A manual run is logged in two cases only. One is when a score moves enough to matter. The other is when the last log is over a week old.

The old phrase no longer appears on the Scorecard page, in its HTML or in its markdown.
