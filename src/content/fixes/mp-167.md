The line under "Previous runs" on `/scorecard/` said a run is logged when a score moves enough to matter, or after a week without one. Since 15 November the scheduled run has published every night. The log then showed rows for 15 and 16 November with every metric the same as on 13 November. Neither row moved a score, and neither came a week after the one before. The markdown version of the page carried the same sentence.

This let down a returning reader who reads the history for what changed. The rule sent them looking for a score that moved in those rows, and they found none. The page exists to report on the site truthfully, and a stale rule one line above the log undercut that.

The sentence in `ScorecardBody.astro` now gives the rule the log follows. Every scheduled run is logged. A manual run is logged only when a score moves enough to matter or the last log is over a week old.

The old phrase no longer appears on `/scorecard/`, in the HTML or in the markdown. An unchanged row now reads as a nightly check.
