The accessibility post is about one widget, the filter pills. It said, in the present tense, "My site has two filter widgets", one on /writing and one on /builds. Both were removed in the August redesign.

On the live site, /writing had no filter buttons. /builds redirected to /projects, which had none either. The only filter left was the type filter on /changelog.

The post's case is "go and listen for yourself". A reader who did, and found no controls, had reason to doubt the rest of it.

The line now puts the two widgets in the past tense and says both were removed. The post links to the changelog filter as the one a reader can still try. The old sentence no longer appears on the page.
