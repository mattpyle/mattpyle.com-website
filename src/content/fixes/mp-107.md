The changelog page lets a reader filter by type. Of its entries, 3 are launches, 9 are features, 2 are infrastructure and 1 is an experiment. The changelog feed had 15 items and no category element on any of them.

That let down someone who wants to hear about launches and not every infrastructure tweak. Readers such as Inoreader, Feedly and NetNewsWire can filter on a category. This feed gave them none.

Each feed item now carries its entry's type as a category element, the same type the page filters on.

The feed now holds a category of launch on three items, and every item carries its type.
