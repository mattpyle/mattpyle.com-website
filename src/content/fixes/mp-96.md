The 404 page offers three ways back in. The third sends readers to the changelog, "including pages that moved". No changelog entry records a page moving.

The site has moved two sets of addresses, /builds to /projects and /agent-traffic to /activity. Both redirect, and neither has an entry. A reader who typed a half-remembered post address reached the 404 and followed the link to look for a move that was not there.

The 404 is the one page a lost reader is sure to read. Its suggestions should lead somewhere that answers the question.

The clause is cut from the description. The link now reads "What has shipped on this site." The 404 page no longer contains the claim.
