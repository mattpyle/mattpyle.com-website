The "Rate limits" section of /steward explained the cost of the public audit endpoint. It said that every accepted call spends a dozen requests at somebody else's origin under this site's name.

That held only for the first call about a site. A second call in the same UTC hour was accepted and counted against the rate limit. It returned the first run's stored report and made no requests at the audited site. A test with example.com showed 10 requests on the first call and none on the second.

The page never mentioned this sharing. Yet it is the best answer the endpoint has to the question of whether it is an open relay. A developer copying the design would learn the rate limits and miss the protection for third-party sites.

The paragraph now says that the first fast audit of a site in each UTC hour spends a dozen requests. It adds that later calls that hour get the same report and cost the site nothing. The old sentence is gone.
