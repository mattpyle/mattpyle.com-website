The activity page reports agent traffic to the site. Its first row in the By client table was the site's own nightly auditor, with 626 of 1,512 requests in seven days. That is 41% of the week.

The page never said so. Its text did not name the auditor, and the note on how requests are counted did not mention it.

A peer in the same field comparing numbers would overestimate outside interest by roughly 1.7 times. A peer who spotted the row would discount every figure on the page.

The row now reads as the site's own nightly auditor. The count is unchanged, and the page says whose requests they are.
