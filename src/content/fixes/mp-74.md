The By client table on the Activity page showed a 7-day and a 30-day column. It sorted its rows by an all-time total that it did not show. The visible lead column read 865, 498, 207, 524, 49, 294, and so on.

On a phone only the 7-day column shows, so the order looked random. The table also stops at 25 rows, and it said that 6 further clients were not shown.

The page had just gained a Verified column for signed and IP-verified hits. Those rows were all recent, so they sorted to the bottom, under clients reading 0 and 0. At one reading, the log held 4 IP-verified hits, and the table showed none of them.

A reader returning to see which bots prove who they are saw the least of the newest evidence. The table now sorts by the 7-day column, then the 30-day column, then all-time, before it cuts at 25 rows.
