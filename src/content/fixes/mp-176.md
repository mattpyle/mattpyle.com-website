The "Machine-readable resources" list in `/agents.md` named the sitemap, `/robots.txt`, `/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt`, the WebMCP files and ten discovery documents. It named neither RSS feed. The word "rss" did not appear in the file. Its only mention of feeds was "these text feeds", which named none.

A reader who liked a post might ask their assistant to keep them posted on new writing. The assistant would follow the site's own skill, which says the full inventory of pages and resources lives in agents.md. There it found nothing to subscribe to. `/llms.txt` and `/robots.txt` already listed both feeds, so an agent had to look elsewhere to find them.

The fix added two bullets to the resources list in `public/agents.md`, after `/llms-full.txt`. `/rss.xml` is described as the RSS 2.0 feed of the writing, for following new posts. `/changelog/rss.xml` is described as the RSS 2.0 feed of the changelog, for following what ships on this site. The wording matches `/llms.txt`.

After the change, the resource list in `/agents.md` named both feeds and said what each was for. An agent asked to follow Matt's writing or changes found them in the brief the skill points to.
