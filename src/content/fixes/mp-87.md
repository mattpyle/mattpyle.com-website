The five experiment pages are /steward/, /webmcp/, /scorecard/, /audit/ and /activity/. All five served the same share image. It shows the name "Matt Pyle" and the line "Growth, PLG, and the agentic web".

The three posts and the 14 changelog entries each had their own card with a title and date. The experiment pages are the ones most likely to travel, and they had none.

A peer who shared one of these pages gave their audience a preview that said nothing about why the link was sent.

The image generator now renders a card for each of the five pages. It uses the post-card layout, with an "Experiment" label in place of the date. Each page passes its card as its share image.

None of the five pages now serves the fallback card.
