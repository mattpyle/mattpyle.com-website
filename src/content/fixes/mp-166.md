The About page said "I am not a professional engineer" twice. The second paragraph of the introduction opened with it. Two sections later, the first item under "Some things that are true" said it again: "I am not a professional engineer, and nothing here pretends otherwise." The markdown version at `/about.md` repeated it in the same way.

The page is where a recruiter goes first to judge Matt. Read from the top, the repeat sounds unedited. One of the three things that are true added nothing new to the page.

The list item is gone. The introduction is unchanged and still explains the point in full.

The sentence now appears once on `/about/` and once in `/about.md`. The list holds two items, and neither repeats the introduction.
