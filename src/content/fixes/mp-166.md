The About page said "I am not a professional engineer" twice. The sentence opened the second paragraph of the introduction. A screen further down, it opened the first item under "Some things that are true". The markdown version at `/about.md` held both as well.

Someone weighing Matt for a role often opens the About page first and reads it top to bottom. They met the same disclaimer twice. One of the list's three true things gave them no new fact.

The fix removed the list item from the page and from its markdown version. The introduction is unchanged, so the sentence stays where the introduction explains it.

The sentence now appears once on the About page and once in `/about.md`.
