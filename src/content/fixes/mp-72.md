On a phone, the first screen of the About page showed the opening lede and nothing else about who Matt is. The employer first appeared in body text 1,906 pixels down. "Say hello" began at 2,489 pixels.

The "at a glance" rail came last, at 3,056 pixels on a page 4,235 pixels tall. It holds the headshot, the one-line description and the job title. On a wide screen it sits beside the text.

A recruiter arriving from a link in the LinkedIn app wants to know in seconds who Matt is. One who stops after a screen or two left without the face, the role or the employer.

The rail came after the text in the markup, and the narrow layout set no order. On narrow screens the rail now sits after the lede and before the "This site is an experiment" section. The wide layout is unchanged.
