On every post, the site header marked its "writing" link with `aria-current="page"`. On every changelog entry, the "changelog" link carried the same mark. Each page held it twice, once in the wide navigation and once in the narrow one.

A screen-reader user tabbing through the header on a post heard "writing, current page, link". They were on a post, not on the list of posts. The header is the one landmark they hear on every page. On every post and entry, it gave them the wrong place.

The cause was a prefix match. The header treated any path under `/writing/` as a match for the writing link. It then marked every match as the current page. Now the link is marked `aria-current="page"` only on the exact page it points to. On a post or an entry, the section link is marked `aria-current="true"`, the current item in a set. The underline styles now match either value.

Posts and changelog entries now serve `aria-current="page"` 0 times. The `/writing/` and `/changelog/` pages keep it. The underline that shows sighted visitors their section still appears on posts and entries.
