The changelog entry on the homepage activity section said each row links to the full changelog entry. None of the five rows was a link. Each was plain text, and only "full changelog" linked.

The rows lost their links in a homepage rebuild on 20 August, on purpose. The entry kept describing the old design.

A returning reader who wanted to read a change in full clicked a row and nothing happened. The changelog is the record of what the site does, so a wrong claim there matters more than elsewhere.

The fix removed the sentence from the entry. It added no replacement note. The entry no longer says the rows link.
