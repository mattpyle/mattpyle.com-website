The footer navigation on every page was named "Social links". It held six links and one button. Only three were social profiles: GitHub, LinkedIn and Bluesky. The other four were the RSS feed, the activity page, the WebMCP page and the switch to retro mode.

A screen reader user lists a page's landmarks to find their way. Hearing "Social links navigation", they skip it unless they want a profile. Yet this landmark is the only place on most pages that links to /activity and /webmcp. It also holds the only control for changing appearance.

The fix renames the landmark from "Social links" to "Footer". The name now covers everything inside it. The home page, the About page and the screen readers post all carry the new name, and none carries the old one.
