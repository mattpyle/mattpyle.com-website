On 20 November 2026, the post "Accessibility, AI, and testing with screen readers" gained a new paragraph. It corrects how the filter on `/changelog` works. Every date for the post still said it was last updated on 30 October 2026.

That old date appeared under the title, in the page data and in the markdown copy at `/writing/accessibility-and-ai.md`. The sitemap and the WebMCP index gave it too. Asked how to follow new posts, the site's A2A agent also answered "updated 30 Oct 2026".

A returning reader uses the updated line under the title to decide whether to read a post again. An agent asked what has changed on the site uses each post's modified date the same way. Neither had any reason to reread the post, so both missed the correction.

The fix sets the post's updated date to 20 November 2026, the day the paragraph shipped. Every dated place on the site reads that one field.

The post page and its markdown copy now give 20 November 2026 as the date of the last change.
