Every post has an "On this page" contents block in its side rail. It lists the post's sections as links. None of those links sat in a list. On the Accessibility and AI post, 0 of its 6 contents links were list items. On the WebMCP post, 0 of 9 were.

This let down people who use a screen reader to move through a long post. A screen reader says "list, 6 items" when it enters a list. Its list keys skip past a whole list in one press. Here the reader heard a run of links with no count and no end. They could not tell how long the contents were. They could not jump past them in one keystroke.

The contents links are now an ordered list, with one item per link. The list styling is reset, so it shows no bullets or numbers. The spacing and layout are unchanged on desktop and phone.

On production, all 6 contents links on the Accessibility and AI post now sit in list items. All 9 on the WebMCP post do too. A screen reader can now announce the count and skip the contents. The header navigation and the footer links are still not marked up as lists.
