The sitemap gave the Projects page a last-modified date of 15 July 2026. The page did not exist at that address until 20 August. It gained two project cards on 2 October.

The About page and the homepage carried the same 15 July date. Those dates were written by hand and never moved forward.

A crawler that trusts last-modified has no reason to re-fetch Projects. It would never learn about two of the page's three projects. The site publishes these dates so agents and search engines can trust them, and these ones were plainly wrong.

The dates now read 2 October for Projects and the homepage, and 5 September for About. None of the three shows 15 July.
