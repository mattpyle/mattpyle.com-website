Every post, and most changelog entries, carry a date with no time. The feeds turned that into midnight GMT. A reader west of UTC sees midnight GMT as the evening before.

The WebMCP post is dated 3 September, and its feed item read "Thu, 03 Sep 2026 00:00:00 GMT". In Los Angeles or New York that shows as 2 September. All 3 items in the posts feed and 8 of 13 in the changelog feed were a day early.

A subscriber sees the date first, so each new post looked a day older than the page said. The entries with a real publish time were already correct.

A date-only value now becomes 12:00 UTC before it reaches the feed, which is the same calendar day from UTC-11 to UTC+11. The feeds' last-build dates take the same change. Items with a real publish time are left alone.
