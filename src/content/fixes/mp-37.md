On the WebMCP post at 1280 by 800, the rail beside the article was 745px tall. A height cap held it to 688px, so it scrolled inside itself. The last two actions, including the RSS link, sat below its visible edge.

A reader who finished the post and looked to the rail for a way to subscribe did not see it. Only keyboard focus brought the actions into view. Mouse users and sighted readers missed them.

The RSS action was the site's answer to "where do I subscribe from a post?". On the post most likely to bring in a developer, that action was hidden.

The rail no longer has a height cap or its own scrollbar. It flows with the article, and it pins in place only when its full height fits the window. At 1280 by 800 it needs 857px, so it flows and nothing is hidden.
