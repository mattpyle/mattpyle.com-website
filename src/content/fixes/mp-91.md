Two documents describe which MCP revisions the site's /mcp endpoint speaks: agents.md and the discovery document at /.well-known/mcp-server. Both said "2026-07-28, and 2025-06-18 and earlier".

The endpoint also answers 2025-11-25. Its server card lists that revision. A test holds the server card to the list the server serves. Nothing held the prose to it.

An MCP client author who wanted the newest 2025 revision would read the prose, conclude it was unsupported, and fall back to 2025-06-18.

Both sentences now say "2025-11-25 and earlier". The old wording appears in neither file.
