The WebMCP page and the how-to post both told testers to use "Chrome 149 or later". Their snippets pass arguments to executeTool as a plain object. The page's own note says Chrome changed to that form in version 155.

A developer who wants to drive the tools before copying the approach would install the stated version. On Chrome 150 to 154 they could meet the argument error the note describes. Neither page contained the words "Chrome 155".

The testing line on /webmcp and the "Try it on this website!" line in the post now say "Chrome 155 or later".

Both pages now give a minimum version that matches the object-form calls shown beside it.
