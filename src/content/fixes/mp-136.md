The Machine-readable resources section of llms.txt had 15 entries, including scorecard.json and activity.json. It named neither RSS feed. The text "rss.xml" appeared nowhere in the file.

Both feeds exist. The writing feed at /rss.xml has 3 items and the changelog feed has 15. Every page advertises the writing feed, and /writing.md links it. Only the agent index left it out.

A reader who asked their assistant to "follow Matt's writing" sent that agent to llms.txt, where it found no feed to subscribe to.

llms.txt now lists the writing feed at /rss.xml and the changelog feed at /changelog/rss.xml, each with a short note on what it carries. The agent-surfaces answer on /a2a lists them too.
