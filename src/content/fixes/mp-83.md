The writing feed at /rss.xml is titled as the site's writing. Its channel link, which a feed reader shows as the feed's home, pointed at the homepage.

A subscriber who clicked the feed's title in their reader wanted the list of posts. They landed on the homepage instead. The changelog feed had the same fault and already pointed at its own list. The writing feed had not been given the same change.

The fix passes the writing list as the feed's site address, as the changelog feed does. Item links are root-relative, so each post link still resolves to its own address under /writing/.

The channel link now reads https://www.mattpyle.com/writing/.
