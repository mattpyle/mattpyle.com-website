The word "Matt" appeared nowhere in the main content of a changelog entry. The only identity on screen was the site's logo. The name sat in the page title and in structured data, which a visitor does not see.

Writing posts already show "Matt Pyle" in their side rail. The three posts carry it. None of the 14 changelog entries did.

Entries are often the first page someone sees, because each has its own share card. They also describe experiments in the first person without saying whose site it is.

The entry's meta line now names "Matt Pyle", linked to the About page, before the date. On the reading-budget entry, the main content now includes the name.
