The retro home page has an "Email the Webmaster" panel. A visitor types a note and presses SEND IT. The page files the note in the browser and shows a 207-character confirmation.

Two things went wrong for a screen reader user. The form that held focus disappeared, so focus dropped to the page body. And the confirmation sat in the reading order twice: once as the visible text and once in a hidden live region that nothing ever emptied. Reading the panel line by line, they heard the whole joke twice, on either side of the LinkedIn link.

The change does two things after a note is filed. It empties the hidden live region once it has spoken. It also moves focus to the visible confirmation, which can take focus without joining the tab order.

Now the confirmation appears once in the reading order, and focus sits on it. The next Tab lands on the LinkedIn link.
