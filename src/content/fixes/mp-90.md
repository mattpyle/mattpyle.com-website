The changelog entry at `/changelog/agent-reading-budget/` is titled "Steward measures an agent's reading budget". Its body never said what Steward is. None of its five links went to the Steward page. It is the only changelog entry that names Steward.

A new visitor arriving from a shared link reads the title and looks for what Steward is. The entry gave them no answer, so they could not judge it. The page that explains Steward was one link away.

The fix adds Steward to the sentence that says the site now measures the reading budget. That mention links to `/steward/` and describes Steward as the site's editorial agent and audit runner.

The entry now links to the Steward page and says what Steward is in the same sentence.
