The changelog feed is titled "Matt Pyle: Changelog". Its channel link, the address a feed reader treats as the feed's home, was the site's homepage.

A reader who subscribed from the changelog page and then used "visit website" in their feed reader went to the homepage. They wanted the whole log.

The cause was how the feed was built. It passed the site's root address as the channel link. The item links were already full addresses, so only the channel link was wrong.

The channel link now points to the changelog page. The item links and the feed's own address are unchanged, and the writing feed still links to the homepage.
