The "Content structure" list in `agents.md` was the site's map of its pages for agents. It named eleven paths, from `/` to `/webmcp` and `/steward`. It did not name `/fix-log` or `/audit`. The site's `llms.txt` listed both pages under "Pages".

The site told agents to read `agents.md` for how to cite it. An agent that read it got an incomplete map of the pages. It might not cite the Fix log or the Audit page. The two files the site gave agents also disagreed on which pages existed.

The fix added two lines to the list in `public/agents.md`. The `/fix-log` line described the log and said where each fix's page was, with its raw-markdown copy at `/fix-log/<slug>.md`. The `/audit` line described the form that ran a fast agent-readiness audit on a named site.

After the fix, the list in `agents.md` named thirteen paths, including `/fix-log` and `/audit`.
