Walking /webmcp with the keyboard gives 61 focus stops. Four of them had no role and no name. They were the scroll wrappers around the Input, Type, Required and Constraints tables of get_recent_writing, search_content, set_appearance and sign_guestbook.

On the same page, the snippet and output boxes were already named, for example "Output from" a tool. Only the four input tables were silent.

A screen-reader user tabbing through the tool catalogue met four stops in the middle of the site's main agent experiment with nothing to say.

Each wrapper now has a region role and a label of the form "Inputs for" followed by the tool's name. The table inside keeps its table semantics.

All four focusable input tables now carry a label.
