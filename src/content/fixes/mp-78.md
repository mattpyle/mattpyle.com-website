On the audit page, a visitor types an address and presses Enter. The form posts and the whole page reloads. Focus returns to the top, and the status message in the form is gone with the old page.

What a screen reader announces on load is the page title. That title was the same before and after: the empty form's. The report was on the page, but nothing told the visitor it existed.

The failure pages shared the title as well. It is also how a visitor tells apart several report tabs.

The title is now built from the page's state. A report is titled "Audit of" and the audited host. A failure carries its own heading. The empty form keeps its title.
