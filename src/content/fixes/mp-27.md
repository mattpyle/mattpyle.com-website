The Projects page described the site's method as hypothesis-first. Its card said so, and the entry body said every change is tracked against a written hypothesis first.

A developer who wanted to copy the practice went looking for the hypotheses. Only 2 of the 13 changelog entries state one. No page publishes a log of surprises or an inconclusive result.

The claim was the most interesting line on the page, and the evidence did not back it. A reader who checked would trust the rest of the page less.

The fix removed the phrase from the card and the sentence from the entry body. It added no replacement caveat. The page and the full-text version of the site no longer make the claim.
