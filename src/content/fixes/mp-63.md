In retro mode, /steward draws a 2px solid frame around two tables: the facts about one audit request, and the limits table. The cells kept the horizontal padding of the modern look, where there is no frame. So the text sat on the frame.

At 1280 by 800, the row headers such as "User-Agent" touched the left frame at 0px. The limits table measured 0px on the right as well, and the facts table 3.7px. The five retro tables on /activity leave 7 to 8px on both sides.

A visitor reading the page that introduces the site's own audit saw text that looked clipped. Retro is a showcase appearance, and agents can switch it on.

The fix gives both tables the end-cell padding the /activity tables already use: 8px on the first cell and the last cell of each row. Text in both tables now keeps its distance from the frame on both sides.
