A changelog entry showed one date in its head: the day it shipped. Ten of the 15 entries had been revised later. The later date reached the page's metadata but not the page.

The scorecard-run-history entry carries a "Correction" paragraph dated 11 September, yet its head showed only the July ship date. Writing posts already show "updated" beside their date.

A returning reader opens the changelog to see what changed. They had no sign that an entry was revised.

An entry whose updated date differs from its ship date now shows "updated" and that date beside the ship date. It follows the same rule as the writing posts.

The markdown-post-variants and scorecard-run-history entries both show their updated dates.
