On 17 November the site's WebMCP origin-trial token expired. The site's WebMCP tools stopped registering until the token was renewed later that day. That night's run on `/scorecard` still scored Agentic Browsing a Pass, at 4 of 4. Its row read "Agentic Browsing fell from 6/6 to 4/4; all 107 pages passed all four public metrics." The runs on 16 and 18 November listed 6 of 6.

The two WebMCP checks did not fail that night. They applied on no page, so they left the total. A returning reader reads the newest rows to see what moved and why. They saw a fall beside a claim that every page passed. Nothing on the page said WebMCP was down. The scorecard is the site's evidence that it is agent-ready, and it published this outage as a pass.

The run's commentary now compares this run's Agentic Browsing checks with the last published run's. When a check that was counted before applies on no page now, the commentary names it. That sentence replaces the bare "fell from 6/6 to 4/4". The 17 November row was corrected to the same wording.

The 17 November row now names WebMCP and WebMCP form coverage as the checks that applied on no page. The score still reads 4 of 4. The old sentence no longer appears on `/scorecard`.
