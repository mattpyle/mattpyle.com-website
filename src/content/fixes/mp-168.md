On 17 Nov the site's WebMCP origin-trial token expired, and its WebMCP tools stopped registering. That night's run on the Scorecard page still scored Agentic Browsing 4 of 4, with a Pass. Its row read "Agentic Browsing fell from 6/6 to 4/4; all 107 pages passed all four public metrics."

A returning reader reads the newest rows under "Previous runs" to see what moved and why. From this row they could not tell that WebMCP was down. The runs on 16 and 18 Nov count six checks, including WebMCP and WebMCP form coverage. The 17 Nov row showed a fall beside a claim that everything passed, and gave no reason.

The cause was in how the score is counted. A check that applies on no page leaves the total instead of failing. With no tools registered, the two WebMCP checks applied nowhere, so 4 of 4 read as a Pass.

The fix changes how a run's commentary is written. When a check the last published run counted applies on no page now, the commentary names that check. It no longer reports a bare fall in the ratio. The 17 Nov row was corrected to the same wording.

The row now says that WebMCP and WebMCP form coverage applied on no page. It says the 4 of 4 covers only the remaining checks. The old sentence no longer appears on the Scorecard page.
