Both "Hello, World!" and the post on accessibility, AI and screen readers showed "updated 07 Aug 2026" in their headers. The same date sat in each post's structured data as its last-modified date.

Nothing a reader could see had changed on that day. Two edits touched the posts. One changed only the search title and description. The other added trailing slashes to internal link addresses. The last visible change was the publish date: 18 July for "Hello, World!" and 31 July for the screen readers post.

A returning reader takes the updated date as a sign that a post is worth reading again. A false date teaches them to ignore it. Search engines and agents read the same false freshness from the structured data.

The fix set each post's updated date back to its last visible change. "Hello, World!" now says 18 July and the screen readers post says 31 July, in the header and in the structured data. The 7 August date is gone from both.
