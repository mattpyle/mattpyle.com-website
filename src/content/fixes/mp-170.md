The post on accessibility and AI said the site's filters settled on buttons, with the active one marked `aria-current="true"`. It said radio patterns were turned down because of their arrow-key contract. On 21 July the filter component was rebuilt as a radio group with arrow keys. The post was not updated, and its markdown named `radiogroup` 0 times.

The post also tells readers that the one filter left is the type filter on /changelog. A screen-reader user who followed that pointer found six radio buttons in a group, each with `aria-checked`. The arrow keys moved the selection. No filter on the site used `aria-current`. The post's account of its own pattern did not match the filter it pointed to.

The fix adds one sentence after the "settled" paragraph in `src/content/writing/accessibility-and-ai.md`. The old paragraph stays as the history. The new sentence says the filter became a radio group with `aria-checked` and arrow keys on 21 July. It also says the filter on /changelog uses that pattern today.

The post on production now names the radio group in both its page and its markdown version. Its account ends on the pattern a reader meets on /changelog.
