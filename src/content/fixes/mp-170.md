The post on accessibility and AI explained how its filters ended up. It said they settled as buttons marked with `aria-current="true"`. It also said radio patterns were passed over because of their arrow-key contract.

That account was written for the July version. On 21 July the filter component was rebuilt as a `role="radiogroup"` of `role="radio"` buttons. Each carries `aria-checked`, and the arrow keys move the selection. No filter on the site uses `aria-current` any more.

The post points readers to the one filter left, on /changelog. A screen-reader user who went there heard a radio group, not the current-marked buttons the post described. Pressing the right arrow moved the selection from "all" to "launch". The post is about checking a page's claims by listening, so this mismatch is the first thing a careful reader would hear.

The post now keeps the old paragraph as history. A new sentence follows it. It says the filter became a radio group with arrow keys on 21 July, and that /changelog uses it today. The post's markdown on production now names `radiogroup`.
