The post on accessibility and AI is the oldest on the site. It ended on its last paragraph, and then the footer began. The footer links to the feed, GitHub, LinkedIn, Bluesky, activity and WebMCP, but to no other post.

The post template passed only the next older post to each page. The oldest post had none, so the row was not drawn. The other two posts never pointed to a newer one.

This post is the one most likely to be found through search. Its last line asks the reader to go and listen to a screen reader. A visitor arriving there from a search result had no way to a second page.

Every post now ends with a newer and older pair, as changelog entries already did. The oldest post links to the next newer one.
